Privacy notice

How we handle your information

Last updated 19 November 2026

Who we are

Iruobe Ayomide Co. is an online retail business operated by Iruobe Akhigbe Ayomide in Lagos, Nigeria. For anything about your data, email iruobeakhigbe@gmail.com.

What this website collects

  • This site has no accounts, no cookies and no analytics or tracking.
  • The supplier enquiry form doesn't send anything to us by itself. It opens your own email app with your details filled in, and we only receive what you choose to send.
  • Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts and the site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web server, these services may see your IP address when your browser loads the page.

How we use what you send us

We use the details in your email to reply to you, assess a possible supplier relationship, and keep normal business records such as orders and invoices. We don't sell your information or share it with anyone for marketing.

How long we keep it

We keep correspondence for as long as we need it for the business relationship and our records. If you'd like us to delete your details and we're not required to keep them, just ask.

Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and other laws that may apply to you, you can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or object to how we use it. Email us and we'll respond promptly.
