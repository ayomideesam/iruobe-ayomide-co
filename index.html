Our standards

A small business with strict standards.

01

Authentic, always

We only buy new, sealed stock from sources we can verify, and keep a supplier invoice for every unit we sell. Nothing used, refurbished or grey-market is ever sold as new.

02

Delivered by Amazon

Our inventory is stored in Amazon fulfilment centres in the US, so orders ship fast and returns follow Amazon's customer-first policies.

03

Fair pricing

We price competitively and never inflate prices on hard-to-find or high-demand items. A good deal for the customer is a repeat customer.

How we work

From supplier to doorstep in four careful steps.

    Source

    We research real customer demand, then buy from manufacturers, authorised distributors and reputable retailers.

  2. 2

    Inspect & prepare

    A US-based prep partner checks every shipment, then labels and packs it to marketplace standards.

  3. 3

    Fulfil

  4. 4

    Support

Supplier FAQ

Questions brands usually ask.

Where do you sell?

We sell to customers in the United States on Amazon.com, using Fulfilment by Amazon. Amazon.ca and Walmart Marketplace are next on our roadmap.

Will you follow our MAP and brand policies?

Yes. We list at or above your minimum advertised price, use your approved content and imagery, and follow any reseller guidelines you share with us.

Where would our orders ship?

To our US-based prep partner, who inspects each shipment and sends it on to Amazon fulfilment centres. We share the full address when we open an account with you.

What size orders do you place?

We usually start with a test order, then reorder steadily as products sell through. If you have a minimum order quantity or opening order value, let us know up front.

What are your payment terms?

We're happy to prepay initial orders. Once we've built a track record together, we're open to discussing terms.
